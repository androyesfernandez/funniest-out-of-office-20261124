# Duel test — The Funniest Out-of-Office

16 drafts, each verified by script (node string length). Judged pairwise
against the criteria in order: (1) a real one-line out-of-office a person
would set and send; (2) genuine surprise / twist; (3) own voice, not a
template; (4) fresh, not the joke from a thousand inboxes; (5) every word
earns its place.

## Bracket

Round of 16: C1 > C9 (fern's concrete payoff beats the goldfish); C2 > C15
(meta shrug beats the thin drawer joke); C3 > C8 (restraining order beats
one-note fishing); C4 > C12 ("define urgent" triple beat beats the sea);
C5 > C13 ("Future Me" list beats the gentler charger parallel); C6 > C14
(unionised inbox is fresher than the known auto-reply-yes shape); C7 > C11
(two beats beat the familiar cat-on-keyboard); C10 > C16 (Roomba vacuuming
email beats the boundaries framing).

Quarterfinals: C1 > C2 (concrete fern scene beats the meta shrug);
C3 > C4 (sharper twist than "define urgent"); C6 > C5 (fresher than the
travel-OOO list); C10 > C7 (one vivid payoff beats two beats, and C7's fern
promotion repeats C1).

Semifinals: C1 > C3 (the numbered payoff "approved three of your emails
and watered none of them" lands harder than "I support it"); C10 > C6
(the Roomba's physical absurdity is more visual than the abstract union).

Final: C10 > C1. Both read like a real out-of-office with a deadpan voice
and a tight payoff. The Roomba wins on freshness: plant-watching-inbox is a
familiar OOO joke shape, while an intern who is a Roomba vacuuming your
email is a more original image, and every word in C10 earns its place at
125 chars.

## Winner

**C10 (125 chars):** "Out of office until Monday. I left an intern in charge
of my inbox. The intern is a Roomba. It has vacuumed your email twice."
